## Issue 1 draft: Offshore hiring in 12 questions

Before you sign with any offshore studio, ask twelve questions. The five that matter most:

1. Who exactly will build my product, and can I meet them?
2. Who owns the code, designs and content, and where is it in the contract?
3. What overlap hours will we have, and how will I see progress?
4. How do you test, review and handle defects after launch?
5. How can I scale down or leave, and what do I get on exit?

Vague answers are the clearest warning sign. The full list, with what a good answer sounds like, is here: https://www.throughout.io/insights/hiring-offshore-developers-12-questions
