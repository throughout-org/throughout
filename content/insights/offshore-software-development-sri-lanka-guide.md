Offshore software development in Sri Lanka means hiring an engineering studio or dedicated developers based in Colombo to design, build and maintain your software, usually at lower cost than an onshore agency in Australia, Europe or the Gulf, with a working-day overlap that makes daily collaboration practical.

This guide explains how that works in practice, what to check before you sign, and when offshore is the wrong answer. Throughout is a Colombo studio, so we have an obvious interest. We have tried to write the guide we would want to read as a founder.

## What does offshore software development involve?

Offshore development is a delivery arrangement, not a product. You keep the business goal and the decisions, while the offshore team supplies engineers, designers, QA and usually a delivery lead who turn that goal into working software, often at lower cost and with access to a wider talent pool.

There are three common shapes:

- **Project team:** a team delivers a defined website, app or MVP against a scope and timeline.
- **Dedicated team:** developers work inside your tools and roadmap for months or years.
- **Staff augmentation:** individual engineers fill gaps in a team you already manage.

Our separate guide to [dedicated teams versus project teams](/insights/dedicated-team-vs-project-team-offshore) covers how to choose between the first two.

## Why do founders choose Sri Lanka?

Sri Lanka has a large English-speaking technical workforce, a mature web and mobile development scene centred on Colombo, and offshore rates that are typically lower than local agencies in the markets it serves. The practical advantages usually come down to four things:

- **English as a working language** for specifications, standups and documentation.
- **A useful time zone.** Sri Lanka is UTC+5:30 all year with no daylight saving, so overlap changes only when your country moves its clocks.
- **Boutique studios** where you can talk to the people doing the work instead of an account layer.
- **Cost.** Rates vary by seniority and studio, so compare written quotes rather than headline hourly numbers.

## How much working-day overlap will you get?

Sri Lanka is UTC+5:30 all year, so overlap depends on your own clocks. Assuming a working day of roughly 9:30 to 18:30 in Colombo, the table shows the time difference and approximate shared hours with typical 9 to 5 hours in each market, before daylight saving changes.

| Your market | Time difference | Typical shared hours |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gulf (UAE, UTC+4) | Sri Lanka is 1.5 hours ahead | Most of the working day |
| United Kingdom | 4.5 to 5.5 hours | About 4 to 5 hours |
| Central Europe | 3.5 to 4.5 hours | About 4 to 6 hours |
| Sydney and Melbourne | 4.5 to 5.5 hours | About 2 to 3 hours |

Daylight saving moves these numbers twice a year, so check your own dates. Australia has the thinnest overlap, which is why teams that work well across it agree a fixed daily call window and rely on written updates for everything else.

## How do you vet an offshore studio?

Ask for evidence, not adjectives. A good studio can show live work, introduce the people who will build your product, put the proposal in writing and explain how ownership and delivery are handled. Use this checklist when you compare studios:

1. **Live work you can open.** Ask which products they built, which they run, and what your contact can say about them.
2. **The people you will work with.** Meet the lead engineer, not only the salesperson.
3. **A written proposal** with team, scope, price, payment terms and the assumptions behind the estimate.
4. **Ownership terms.** Code, designs and content should belong to you, agreed in the contract before work starts. Ask for an NDA before you share anything sensitive.
5. **Delivery rhythm.** How often will you see working software, and how are changes handled?
6. **Honest answers about risk.** A studio that never says "that is harder than it looks" is not listening.

## Where does offshore work well?

Offshore works best when the work is well defined and the owner decides quickly. It suits MVPs, marketing sites, e-commerce stores and internal tools, and teams that need steady engineering capacity without hiring locally. Look for these conditions in your own project:

- Products with a clear owner who can make decisions within a day.
- Defined builds such as MVPs, marketing sites, e-commerce stores and internal tools.
- Teams that need continuous engineering capacity without local hiring.
- Work where written specs, design files and tickets are already normal.

## Where is offshore a poor fit?

Offshore is a poor fit when the work cannot leave your jurisdiction, nobody can own priorities, or requirements change daily without documentation. It also struggles where frequent on-site presence is essential. Watch for these warning signs before you commit to any studio:

- Highly regulated work that must stay in your jurisdiction.
- Products where nobody on your side can own priorities.
- Projects where requirements change daily and nobody documents them.
- Anything that needs frequent on-site presence.

## What goes wrong, and how do you prevent it?

Most failed offshore projects fail for ordinary reasons: unclear scope, slow feedback, and no shared definition of done. The fixes are equally ordinary. Write acceptance criteria, agree a feedback turnaround, review working builds weekly and keep the backlog short. ## Frequently asked questions

### Is Sri Lanka good for outsourcing software development?

Yes, for the right work. Sri Lanka suits founders and agencies who want English-speaking engineers, a workable overlap with Australia, Europe and the Gulf, and the ability to talk directly to a small team. It is a smaller talent pool than India, so very large teams can take longer to assemble.

### How do I hire developers in Sri Lanka?

Either hire through a studio that supplies dedicated developers on a contract, or set up your own entity and hire directly. Most founders start with a studio because it removes legal, payroll and management overhead. See [Throughout's engineering services](/services/engineering).

### How is Sri Lanka different from India or Vietnam?

All three are established offshore destinations with different trade-offs in scale, rates and time zone. We compare them in [Sri Lanka vs India vs Vietnam](/insights/sri-lanka-vs-india-vs-vietnam-offshore-development).

### Who owns the code in an offshore engagement?

You should. Ownership is a contract term, so confirm it in writing before work starts.
