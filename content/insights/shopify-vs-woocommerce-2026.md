Choose Shopify if you want a hosted store that is quick to launch, with security and updates handled for you. Choose WooCommerce if you want full control of the code, hosting and data, already run WordPress, or need heavily custom behaviour and can maintain it. Both can rank well when built properly.

Below is how they differ on the points that usually decide it. We build on both, so the aim is to match the platform to your situation, not to push one.

## How do the platforms compare?

Shopify is a hosted subscription platform that handles hosting and security, while WooCommerce is an open-source WordPress plugin that you host and maintain yourself. That single difference drives most of the contrasts in cost, control and maintenance shown in the table below.

| | Shopify | WooCommerce |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Model | Hosted subscription platform | Open-source plugin for WordPress |
| Setup speed | Fast | Moderate |
| Hosting and security | Handled by Shopify | You or your host |
| Customisation | Themes, apps, custom apps | Full code access |
| Cost structure | Plan fees, apps, possible transaction fees | Hosting, plugins, development, maintenance |
| Maintenance | Low | Higher |
| Content and blog | Adequate | Excellent through WordPress |
| Best for | Fast launches, small teams | Custom needs, content-led stores |

Check current prices and fees on each provider's site, since plans change.

## Which is easier to run day to day?

Shopify is easier for most small teams. Hosting, checkout security, backups and platform updates are included, so there is less to break. WooCommerce asks you to manage hosting, updates, plugin compatibility and backups, which is manageable with a developer or good hosting, and is a real ongoing task without one.

## Which gives more control?

WooCommerce gives full access to code and data, so unusual pricing rules, product types or integrations are straightforward to build. Shopify is flexible through apps and custom apps, but some checkout and data behaviours are constrained by the platform. If your requirements are unusual, test them against Shopify's limits before choosing it.

## How do they compare on cost?

Total cost depends on your catalogue and traffic more than the sticker price. Shopify's costs are plan fees, paid apps and any transaction fees tied to payment choices. WooCommerce's costs are hosting, premium plugins, development and maintenance time. Compare a three-year total, including developer time, rather than the monthly fee.

## Which is better for SEO?

Both can rank well. WooCommerce runs on WordPress, which is strong for content and flexible URLs. Shopify handles technical basics well, with some limits on URL structure. In either case results come from clear product and category pages, structured data, fast pages and useful content. We covered this in the [Müllwood case study](/case-studies/mullwood).

## Which scales better?

Both platforms scale to large catalogues. Shopify scales with less engineering effort because Shopify runs the infrastructure, while WooCommerce scales with the right hosting and caching and gives more room to tune performance. Decide based on your own team's ability to run infrastructure.

## When should you choose each?

Choose Shopify for speed and low maintenance, and WooCommerce for control and customisation. If your requirements are standard and your team is small, Shopify usually wins. If you already run WordPress or need unusual behaviour, WooCommerce usually wins. Use these rules of thumb:

**Choose Shopify when:** you want to launch quickly, have a small team, sell standard products and prefer predictable maintenance.

**Choose WooCommerce when:** you already run WordPress, need deep customisation or content-led selling, or want to own the stack.

**Consider migrating when:** the platform blocks a feature you need, or maintenance costs more than the benefit. Plan redirects and metadata carefully so rankings survive; see [redesigning without losing clarity](/insights/how-to-redesign-a-website-without-losing-clarity).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Shopify or WooCommerce better in 2026?

Neither is better for everyone. Shopify suits fast launches and low maintenance. WooCommerce suits custom needs and full control. Decide using your team, requirements and three-year cost.

### Is WooCommerce cheaper than Shopify?

It can be cheaper in plan fees, but you pay for hosting, plugins and maintenance. Compare total cost over several years, including developer time.

### Which platform is better for SEO?

Both can rank well. WooCommerce has an edge for content-heavy sites on WordPress, and Shopify handles technical basics with less effort. Execution matters more than the platform.

### Can I move from WooCommerce to Shopify, or back?

Yes. Products, customers and orders can be migrated, but you must plan URL redirects, metadata and structured data to protect rankings.
