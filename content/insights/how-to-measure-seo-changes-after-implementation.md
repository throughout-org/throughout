An SEO task is not complete when a ticket is closed. First confirm that the intended change reached the live page. Then observe whether the relevant search and business outcomes changed.

Those are two different questions: **Did we implement it correctly?** and **Did it help?**

## Capture the baseline before deployment

Record the affected URLs, query group, impressions, clicks, position range, conversions and measurement dates. Save the original page output and the evidence that motivated the change.

Use a query group rather than one keyword whenever the page addresses several related searches. One ranking can move because of location, personalization or ordinary volatility.

## Validate the release immediately

After deployment, check that:

- The intended content and metadata are present in rendered HTML
- The page returns the expected status code
- Canonical and robots directives are correct
- Structured data remains valid and matches visible content
- Internal links reach the preferred URL
- The sitemap and redirects reflect any approved URL changes
- Analytics and conversion tracking still work

This stage measures implementation quality, not ranking impact.

## Annotate other changes

Record the release date alongside migrations, campaigns, pricing changes, major content updates and known tracking interruptions. Search demand and conversion behavior may change for reasons unrelated to the SEO task.

## Choose an appropriate observation period

There is no universal number of days. A frequently crawled page may show technical changes quickly, while demand and competitive movement require longer observation. Seasonality also determines whether a week-over-week comparison is meaningful.

Define the review date before launch and avoid repeatedly changing the window after seeing the result.

## Compare the right outcomes

Technical fixes may be evaluated through crawl and indexation coverage. Content improvements may use relevant impressions, clicks, landing-page engagement and qualified conversions. Ecommerce work may also consider product discovery and revenue, with appropriate attribution limits.

Do not treat increased impressions as success if the page appears for irrelevant searches. Do not treat a ranking increase as business impact if qualified actions decline.

## Report uncertainty

SEO measurement is observational. Describe what changed, what remained stable and which competing explanations exist. When possible, compare affected pages with a reasonable control group or stagger implementation.

The purpose of measurement is not to manufacture certainty. It is to produce enough evidence to keep, improve or reverse the change responsibly.
