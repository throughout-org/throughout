There is no single best country to outsource software development. India offers the largest talent pool, Vietnam is a fast-growing engineering market with a different time zone, and Sri Lanka offers a smaller, English-speaking market with boutique studios and a workable overlap with Australia, Europe and the Gulf. The right choice depends on team size, time zone and how closely you want to work with the people building your product.

**Disclosure:** Throughout is based in Colombo, so we are not neutral. We have kept the comparison to things you can verify, and we have avoided quoting rates, which vary too much by city, seniority and studio to compare fairly in a table.

## How do the three compare?

The three countries differ most in scale, time zone and typical studio size. India has the largest talent pool, Vietnam is closest to Australia in time zone, and Sri Lanka offers smaller boutique studios with English-language collaboration. Treat the table as a starting hypothesis, not a verdict.

| Factor | Sri Lanka | India | Vietnam |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Time zone | UTC+5:30 | UTC+5:30 | UTC+7 |
| Talent pool size | Smaller | Very large | Large and growing |
| Typical studio size | Boutique and small | From boutique to very large | Small to large |
| English in daily work | Widely used | Widely used | Varies more by team |
| Overlap with Australia | About 2 to 3 hours | About 2 to 3 hours | More, because it is closer |
| Overlap with Europe | Good in mornings | Good in mornings | Narrower |
| Best for | Small to mid teams, close collaboration | Large teams, wide specialisms | Engineering-heavy teams working with Asia-Pacific hours |

Use this as a starting hypothesis, then test it with the actual studios you are considering.

## Why might you choose Sri Lanka?

Sri Lanka suits founders who want a small team and direct contact with the engineers. It works well for MVPs, product websites, e-commerce and SaaS builds for Australia, Europe and the Gulf, where a morning-to-afternoon overlap is enough and English-language specs matter. Consider it when:

- You want a **small team and direct contact** with the engineers.
- You need **English-language** specs and standups without friction.
- Your market is Australia, Europe or the Gulf, and a **morning-to-afternoon overlap** is enough.
- Your project is an MVP, a product website, an e-commerce store or a SaaS build rather than a very large programme.

## Why might you choose India?

India suits projects that need many engineers quickly or niche specialists. Its ecosystem of agencies, freelancers and product companies is the deepest of the three, though quality varies widely, so vendor selection takes more effort on your side. Consider it when:

- You need **many engineers quickly** or niche specialists.
- You value a deep ecosystem of agencies, freelancers and product companies.
- You are comfortable with more vendor selection effort because quality varies widely.

## Why might you choose Vietnam?

Vietnam suits engineering-heavy teams that want a time zone closer to Australia and Asia-Pacific hours. It has a strong and growing engineering market, though English proficiency should be confirmed with the actual team you will work with. Consider it when:

- Your team works on **Asia-Pacific hours** and wants a time zone closer to Australia.
- You want a strong engineering market and are happy to confirm English proficiency in the actual team.
- Your product is engineering-heavy and you will manage requirements in writing.

## How should you decide?

Decide by fixing your time-zone needs first, then team size, then the actual people and written proposals. Daily overlap matters more than a small difference in rates, and a short paid pilot reveals more than any sales call. Follow these five steps:

1. **Fix the time zone first.** Daily overlap matters more than a small difference in rates.
2. **Decide how big the team must become.** If you may need 30 engineers, favour the largest pools.
3. **Interview the actual team,** not the sales lead, in every country.
4. **Compare written proposals** with scope, assumptions and ownership terms.
5. **Run a paid pilot** of two to four weeks before a long commitment.

Regardless of country, the main drivers of success are the same: clear scope, fast feedback and one accountable owner. Our [offshore development guide for Sri Lanka](/insights/offshore-software-development-sri-lanka-guide) covers vetting and risk in more detail.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Sri Lanka cheaper than India for software development?

Rates overlap heavily and depend on the studio, city and seniority. Do not choose a country on a headline rate. Compare written proposals for the same scope.

### Is Sri Lanka good for outsourcing web development compared to India or Vietnam?

Yes for small and mid-sized teams that value English-language collaboration and direct access to engineers. For very large teams, India's talent pool is bigger.

### Which country has the best time zone for Australia?

Vietnam is closest in this comparison. Sri Lanka and India share a time zone with each other and offer a shorter but workable overlap with Australia.

### What about offshore versus local developers?
