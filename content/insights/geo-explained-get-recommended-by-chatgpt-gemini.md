Generative engine optimisation (GEO) is the practice of making your business easy for AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity to find, understand and cite. It builds on SEO fundamentals, adds clear answer-first content and entities, and relies on trustworthy mentions elsewhere. No one can guarantee a recommendation.

Here is what you can control, a checklist, and how to track progress honestly. For the wider comparison with SEO and AEO, see [SEO, AEO and GEO: what changes](/insights/geo-vs-seo-what-actually-gets-you-cited-by-chatgpt-in-2026).

## How do AI assistants choose what to recommend?

AI assistants combine what a model learned in training with live search results and retrieved pages, then write an answer and sometimes cite sources. Pages that are crawlable, clearly written and supported by independent mentions are easier to retrieve and trust. The exact weighting is not public and changes, so treat any claim of a secret formula with caution.

## What can you actually control?

You control access, clarity and evidence. Access means the right crawlers can reach your pages. Clarity means each page states plainly what it is and who it is for. Evidence means your claims are specific, sourced and backed by third parties. You do not control the model or how it ranks, which is why consistent fundamentals beat tricks.

## What is the GEO checklist?

1. **Allow the crawlers you want.** Check robots.txt for the search and user agents of the assistants you care about, for example OAI-SearchBot and ChatGPT-User for OpenAI, PerplexityBot and Perplexity-User, Claude-SearchBot and Claude-User, and Google's controls. Blocking training crawlers and allowing search crawlers are separate choices.
2. **Keep pages indexable.** Fix noindex mistakes, broken canonicals, thin duplicates and slow templates.
3. **Write the answer first.** Put a direct 40 to 60 word answer under each heading, then the detail.
4. **Name entities clearly.** State who you are, where you operate, what you offer and who owns which brand, in text and in schema.org markup.
5. **Use structured data that matches the page.** Organization, Service, FAQPage and BreadcrumbList are common. Google states that no special markup is required for its AI features, so use schema to clarify, not to game.
6. **Publish comparisons and definitions.** Tables and plain definitions are easy to quote.
7. **Earn independent mentions.** Directories, press, partner sites and reviews give assistants corroboration beyond your own site.
8. **Add an llms.txt file if you wish.** It is a plain summary of who you are and your key pages. Support among major assistants is not confirmed, so treat it as low-cost and optional, not a ranking lever.

## Does schema markup get you cited?

Schema helps machines understand a page, but it is not a guarantee of citation. Google says its AI features use the same foundations as search and need no special markup. Use accurate schema because it improves clarity and eligibility, and do not mark up content that is not visible on the page.

## How do you track whether it is working?

Build a fixed set of 10 to 15 buyer prompts, such as "best offshore software development companies in Sri Lanka", and run them monthly in ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity in clean sessions. Record whether you are mentioned, cited with a link and who else appears.

Track the trend, not single results, because answers vary between runs. Tools such as Rankout, Ahrefs Brand Radar or Semrush can automate part of this.

## What does GEO not do?

GEO does not replace SEO, fix a weak product or produce results in days. It will not stop an assistant from sometimes getting facts wrong, so keep your key facts consistent and easy to verify. ## Frequently asked questions

### What is generative engine optimisation (GEO)?

GEO is the practice of structuring content, technical access and third-party evidence so AI assistants can find, understand and cite a business. It builds on SEO and focuses on answers, entities and trust.

### How do I get my business recommended by ChatGPT?

Allow OpenAI's search crawler, keep your pages indexable, publish clear answer-first content, state your entities in schema and earn independent mentions. There is no way to guarantee a recommendation.

### Is GEO different from SEO?

Mostly it is SEO with more attention to direct answers, entity clarity and citations. The foundations of crawlability, quality content and reputation are shared.

### Do I need an llms.txt file?

It is optional. It is a cheap, plain summary for AI crawlers, but its use by major assistants is not confirmed, so do not rely on it.

### How long does GEO take to show results?

Expect months. Crawling, indexing and third-party mentions take time, so track a fixed prompt set monthly and judge the trend.
