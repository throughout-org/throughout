# Pilot article research record

Research date: 6 October 2026

These drafts are deliberately stored outside the Throughout Insights publishing path. Their approved canonical homes are the product domains.

## RankOut: How to Prioritize SEO Issues After an Audit

- Intent: the reader already has an audit and needs an actionable prioritization method.
- SERP pattern: audit checklists, impact/effort matrices, and tool-led guides. Much ranking content explains how to find issues but gives limited treatment to business value, confidence, ownership, dependencies, acceptance criteria and measurement.
- Content gap used: an implementation-ready framework that distinguishes blockers from improvements and closes the validation loop.
- Product claim boundary: uses RankOut’s publicly stated research → decide → approve → implement → validate → measure workflow. It does not reuse illustrative dashboard metrics as customer evidence.
- Primary sources: Google Search Central canonical and robots guidance; Google Search Console Performance documentation.
- Commercial path: invite readers with an existing audit to a product walkthrough rather than interrupting the guide with repeated sales language.

## Comflo: How to Manage WhatsApp Orders Without Spreadsheets

- Intent: a growing seller is losing operational control between WhatsApp chats and a spreadsheet.
- SERP pattern: lightweight order-form tools, WhatsApp automation vendors, shared-inbox products and forum discussions about missing addresses, variants, payment confirmation and ownership.
- Content gap used: a vendor-neutral operating model covering order states, confirmation, assignment, payment verification, exceptions and migration.
- Product claim boundary: shared inbox, order management, customer retention, bulk messaging and product management are treated as live because the product page says they are live.
- Unresolved claim: the Comflo homepage currently mentions Shopify both as a connection and as “coming soon.” The article therefore makes no claim that Shopify is presently available and tells buyers to confirm their exact integration during onboarding.
- Primary source: Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform documentation. Intercom was used as a concrete explanation of shared-inbox behavior.
- Commercial path: one contextual CTA after the operational framework.

## Checkout: How to Choose a POS System in Sri Lanka

- Intent: a Sri Lankan retail buyer is comparing systems and needs a requirements checklist, not another “top five” affiliate-style list.
- SERP pattern: vendor comparison lists, general feature checklists, pricing pages and several recent locally targeted buying guides.
- Content gap used: demonstrable acceptance tests covering offline behavior, exact hardware compatibility, stock movement, exceptions, permissions, backups, migration and exit.
- Product claim boundary: Checkout is described only as an offline-first retail platform currently available for supported macOS setups. Selected barcode scanners and ESC/POS printers require confirmation. Mobile is in development and web is planned, so neither is presented as available.
- Primary sources: Checkout’s live product/availability statements and Shopify’s retail POS hardware and inventory education.
- Commercial path: a requirements discussion for qualified barcode-led retailers, not a claim that Checkout fits every business.
